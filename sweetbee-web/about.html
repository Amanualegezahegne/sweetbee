Our Story

From a Few Hives to a Trusted Name

Sweet Bee Honey began as a small family operation with a simple mission: to share the pure, unfiltered sweetness of nature with our community. What started with just a few hives in the highlands of Tigray has grown into a trusted source of premium honey products loved across Ethiopia.

Every jar we produce carries the same care and passion we had on day one — no shortcuts, no over-processing, just honest honey straight from the hive to your table.

What We Stand For

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

We're dedicated to delivering natural, healthy honey while preserving the environment. We believe every spoonful should be as pure as nature intended — raw, unfiltered, and full of life.

Our Vision

We envision a world where sustainable beekeeping supports both bees and people — where every community has access to pure, traceable honey from healthy, thriving hives.

The Person Behind It All

Meet the Founder

Mahlet Berihun

Founder & CEO

Mahlet founded SweetBee Honey out of a deep love for bees and a desire to provide truly natural products to her community. Raised in Tigray, she grew up watching her family tend to hives and quickly learned that good honey starts with happy bees and healthy land.

How We Grew

Our Journey

2021

Humble Beginnings

Started with 3 hives in Mekele, Tigray. The first jars of golden honey were gifted to neighbours and quickly became the talk of the neighbourhood.

2022

Rapid Expansion

Expanded to 50 hives and started supplying local markets. Community demand confirmed that natural honey could not be replaced.

2023

Going Digital

Opened our first online shop, reaching customers across Ethiopia and enabling direct-to-door delivery of fresh honey products.

2024

Community Roots

Partnered with local farmers to grow sustainably, supporting smallholder beekeepers and creating shared prosperity across the region.

Our Commitment

Built for the Planet

We never over-filter or heat our honey. We preserve enzymes, pollen, and nutrients. Our practices support pollinator health, local ecosystems, and the global bee population.

100% Organic

No pesticides, no additives. Every hive is maintained using certified organic practices that protect the bees and the land they work.

Sustainable Packaging

Our jars and packaging are recyclable or reusable, minimising waste from hive to your home. Small choices, big impact.

Community First

We reinvest in local beekeeping communities, offer training to new farmers, and share profits with the people who make every jar possible.
